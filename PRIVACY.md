# Privacy

IINA Remote doesn't collect, store or share any personal data, and has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

The app talks only to IINA on your own network. It uses Local Network access to find Macs running the
IINA Remote plugin and sends playback commands straight to them. Nothing leaves your network, and there are
no servers of ours involved.

The Apple Watch app reaches IINA through your iPhone. Settings, such as which Mac you picked, stay on your device.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/avr6ude/iina-remote/issues).
